## Overview

A conversational assistant for handling reservations. Instead of filling in forms, users chat with it over iMessage, and a Spring Boot backend does the work behind the conversation through the **Linq iMessage API**.

## How it's built

- **Backend:** a **Spring Boot** service written in **Java** and built with **Maven**.
- **Messaging:** the **Linq iMessage API** connects the service to users' iMessage conversations.

The full source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/dinesh-din/AI-Reservation-assistant).
